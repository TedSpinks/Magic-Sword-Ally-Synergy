## Findings behind the decisions
- **The magic bar is `$0688`** (0-10). Your magic shot (player attack state `$06D0` = `$0A`/`$10`/`$14`) drains it; it refills in roughly 80-100 frames when you do not fire. Pressing Y every 10-40 frames never refills it. [V]
- **The Priest's "shot" is a hit box at the Priest's own position** (the blue sphere around him), built at `$02:AE02-AEB4`. It arms only when the player fires a magic shot, lasts 110 frames (`+3F = $6E`), cannot re-arm while up, and takes its damage from `$02:B1BA[level]`. So it only fires when the bar was charged. [V]
- **Undead multiplier is in the original game** (`$03:80BB`, `$03:8921`): enemy +$50 nonzero and ally = Priest -> damage x16 (or x8 on the second path); ally = Wizard -> halved. Skeleton warriors are flagged. [V]
- **Shot damage tables (base per hit, +1 is added), L1 to L8:** Amazon 12..49 (`$02:820D`), Big Man 30..80 (`$02:8BC9`), Wizard 36..88 (`$02:93CD`), Priest 2..25 (`$02:B1BA`). The Knight/Lizardman/Ninja/Thief tables were not separated cleanly.
- **Ally regen (frames per block, L1..L8, table `$01:8AD6`):** Wizard 1080..240, Priest 1600..300, Ninja 600..320, Amazon 500..290, Thief 500..290, Knight 370..200, Lizardman 300..200, Big Man 200..130 (v10+ slows Big Man x4).
- **Playtest note:** allies level up quickly if you stay with them, so level-speed perks are weak. Saves from the author's RetroArch run on the **FastROM build**; load them into the FastROM ROM, not the clean one.

## Second Wind implementation
- Hook: `$01:9CA8` (player damage). `.normal` path ends in `jml SecondWind` (`$89:A929`). If life went negative, ally = Amazon (`$0424 = 3`), not dying (`$042E = 0`) and `$0772 != floor+1`: set `$0772 = floor+1`, life = (max `$042B` / 16) x (level `$0412` + 3).
- RAM used: `$0771` (Big Man toggle), `$0772` (Second Wind floor stamp), `$0774-$0778` scratch. All verified unreferenced.
- Tested: revive once per floor, second lethal hit dies, new floor re-arms, Priest control dies, both Flicker and Translucent builds.

## Recruiter implementation
- Hook `$01:8457` (new-ally level step of `$01:8432`) -> `RecruitBonus` at `$81:FF80`. While `$0424 == $03` (Amazon) and `$042E == 0`, the table level of the new ally gets +1/+2/+3 by her level `$0412` (0-2 / 3-5 / 6-7), capped at 7 (L8). Scratch byte `$0774`.
- Verified by running the assembled bytes through a small 65816 interpreter (all Amazon levels x incoming levels, non-Amazon, no ally, dying) and by regression of the starting-ally levels in the real game. A live door recruit with an Amazon current ally was not run (the old door-test states no longer drive the door).

## Priest: healer (built in v14)
- Healing items: common heal routine `$02:D64D` hooked at `$02:D64F` -> `PriestHeal` (`$81:FE00`): amount + amount*HealTbl[level]/32 while the Priest is the ally (v116: 4,7,10,13,15,18,21,24 = +12.5% at L1 ... +75% at L8; v14-v115 was (level+1)/8, up to +100%). Item amounts $20/$28 become 36/45 at L1 and 56/70 at L8. [verified by running the assembled bytes in an interpreter; no live item pickup run]
- Life trickle: `JSR $877F` at `$01:DFCA` replaced by `PriestTick` (`$81:FE00+`): +1 life unit every 480, 420, 360, 300, 240, 190, 150, 120 frames (L1-L8), never above max. Against the drain (1 unit per ~183 frames): L6 about even (96%), L7+ net gain. [V in game: 1 unit per 480 frames at L1, per 120 at L8]. For scale, the game's own drain is -8 units per ~1460 frames.
- Priest ally-life regen row (`$01:8B46`) changed from 1600..300 to 960,840,720,600,480,380,300,220 frames per block.
- Scratch RAM `$0774-$077A`. Source: tools/priest.asm, recruit.asm, hack_sw.asm, hackD_sw.asm (build chain: FastROM base -> hack_sw/hackD_sw -> recruit -> bigregen -> priest).

## Wizard: faster magic-bar recharge (built in v15)
- Bar pacing is at `$00:ADF6` (normal) and `$00:AE04` (low life): `$0689 = ($0689+1)&mask`, and when it wraps to 0 the bar (`$0688`, 0-10) steps. The step routine needs `$0689 == 0` at that moment (it compares the 16-bit word `$0688/$0689` with 10), so extra speed must keep that contract; an earlier try that left a remainder in `$0689` let the bar run past 10.
- Perk: with the Wizard as ally (`$0424 = 9`, not dying) extra increments of `$0689` per frame come from a quarter-unit accumulator `$077C` fed by `StepTbl` = 2,3,4,5,11,12,13,14 (levels 1-8). Code at `$82:F200` (tools/wizard.asm).
- Result, frames to refill the bar after a shot (original 55): L1 42, L2 40, L3 33, L4 33, L5 25, L6-8 22. The refill time comes in steps because each bar step also goes through the `$068A` state machine; there is a floor of about 22 frames.
- Verified in game for other allies (55 frames, unchanged), Wizard L1-L8, and the bar stops at 10.
- Warning: free space at `$81:FD00-FDA2` is NOT free (game data); free bank-1 space starts at `$81:FDA3`. Use `$82:F09A+` (3.9 KB) for larger code.

## Ninja: evasion + critical strike (built in v17)
- **Dodge (v31)** (chance per level L1..L8 = 13+7*level of 256: 5%, 8%, 12%, 16%, 19%, 22%, 23%, 24%): hooked at the top of the player-damage routine (`org $019B5B jml DmgEntry`, `org $019B9C jml NinjaDodge`; `NinjaDodge` at `$82:FA00`) so an evade jumps to `$01:9BFB` and skips the invincibility timer, stagger (`$12B6`) and hit sound. It shows EVADED!, sets a 40-frame cooldown (`$077B`) and costs no life. The v17 version lived in AbsorbHook (`.ninja` branch, now `bra .normal`), which still played the hit reaction. RNG: LCG in `$077D` mixed with frame counter `$0601`. Measured: ~17% fewer life drops at L8.
- **Longer invincibility**: the post-hit timer is the player object byte `+$1C` (original `$78` = 120 frames, set at `$01:9BEF`, counted down in the damage routine at `$01:9B5B`, blocks damage while nonzero). Hook `$01:9BEF` -> `NinjaInv` (`$82:F480`): Ninja timer = 150+13*level frames (L1 +25% ... L8 +101%). In seconds (60 fps): original 120 frames = 2.0 s; Ninja L1 150 = 2.5 s, L2 163 = 2.7 s, L3 176 = 2.9 s, L4 189 = 3.1 s, L5 202 = 3.4 s, L6 215 = 3.6 s, L7 228 = 3.8 s, L8 241 = 4.0 s. Real gaps between hits in play run a little longer than the timer (the next hit lands after it expires). To shorten, lower the per-level 13 (e.g. 8 gives 3.4 s at L8). Measured gaps between hits: 120 base, 150 at L1, 242 at L8. (The old note "`$12B6` gate" was wrong; `$12B6` is the player action state.)
- **Critical strike (v31)** (chance L1..L8 = 13+5*level of 256: 5%, 7%, 9%, 11%, 13%, 15%, 17%, 19%): hooked only on the player-weapon damage branches (`org $038052 jml Crit1`, `org $0388B4 jml Crit2`, reading `$069F`), not on the shared sites, so the Ninja's own stars and other ally projectiles never crit (v17 hooked the shared sites `$03:80E3`/`$03:8948` and let ally hits crit). `CritRoll` at `$82:F400`: Ninja active, random < 13+5*level -> `$06A1` doubled (cap 7FFF); CRITICAL! shows on a connecting sword or fire hit.
- Source: tools/ninja.asm, hack_nj.asm, hackD_nj.asm, mfix2.asm (music fix). Build chain: FastROM base -> hack_nj/hackD_nj -> recruit -> bigregen -> priest -> wizard -> mfix2 -> ninja (v21+; v16-v20 used mfix1). Scratch RAM `$077D` (RNG), `$0775`.
- Music fix: **v16-v20 used mfix1.asm (longer frame waits before the upload); that was the wrong fix and only worked by shifting timing. v21 replaces it with mfix2.asm.** Root cause (found Nov 8 on re-check): after a song upload the SPC's resident loader (SPC `$0EEF`, an IPL-protocol clone) finishes with `MOV $F1,#$31` at SPC `$0F35`, which clears the CPU->SPC input ports. The game writes the play command (`$01` to `$2140`, at `$08:8080`) a few microseconds after seeing the loader's final echo. On the slow original ROM that write lands after the clear; with FastROM the CPU is ~33% faster and it sometimes lands before the clear, so the command is wiped (SPC `$0000`, the last command, stays 0) and the floor is silent. Evidence: right after the floor-31 upload the silent and working SPC RAM differ only in driver zero page (`$0000` = 0 vs 1); adding just a JSR/RTS plus 2 NOPs before the write already fixes the author's silent save. mfix2 waits one frame (`JSR $825E`) before writing `$01` (`$08:8080` -> `JSR $F600`).

## On-screen messages (v18 top-right; v22/v23 sprite pop-ups in the score style)
- **EVADED!** (Ninja dodge), **CRITICAL!** (Ninja crit), **REVIVED!** (Amazon Second Wind).
- **Pop-up (v22):** a sprite word drawn in the style of the game's score pop-ups (black outline, dark blue top, light blue middle, white bottom; sprite palette 1, attr `$32`). It appears about 40 px above the object the event happened to (player for EVADED!/REVIVED!, enemy for CRITICAL!), rises 1 px per frame for 40 frames, holds, and disappears after 90 frames (1.5 s). For comparison the game's score pop-ups rise ~1 px/frame for ~30 frames.
- The game has no sprite letters: its score pop-ups are pre-drawn two-digit pairs ("10".."90","00") in ROM at `$14:BCxx-BFxx`, copied into sprite tiles when shown. Our letters (A C D E I L R T V !) are new pixel art in that style; the generator is tools/popfont.py (5x7 strokes, 2 px verticals, outline added automatically, colour by row). Each word is a pre-rendered 64x16 strip (16 tiles, 512 bytes) at `$89:B000` (tools/popgfx.inc).
- Graphics upload: when an event happens, the vblank hook (`MsgHook`, `$00:8A8E`) DMAs that word's tiles (channel 7, four 128-byte chunks) into the **second sprite table**: tiles `$100-$103`, `$110-$113`, `$120-$123`, `$130-$133` (VRAM words `$7000/$7100/$7200/$7300`); the OAM attr has bit 0 set (`$33`). v22 used tiles `$E0-$E7/$F0-$F7`, but the all-floors sweep showed the game uses those on floors 2, 30, 31, 37, 38 and 48, so v23 moved them.
- **All-floors sweep (v23):** tools/floors.py starts any floor 0-55 from the start-point menu (press START, then poke `$0403` = floor every frame for 60 frames while the floor loads). tools/sweepobj.py starts each floor, plays ~80 s (walk right, jump, attack, life refilled) and records which sprite tiles `$C0-$1FF` hold graphics. On a ROM without pop-ups (v21), the only tiles never used on any of the 56 floors were the `$100-$133` 4x4 block, `$0EE`, `$1CF`, `$1DF`. Limit: only the first ~80 s of each floor; bosses deep in a floor may not have been reached.
- Drawing: hook `OamHook` at `$00:95D1` (end of the frame's OAM build, where OAM DMA is armed; OBSEL `$63` = 16x16/32x32, base word `$6000`). While a pop-up shows, it copies the game's OAM (`$1000`, high table `$1200`) into a private buffer at `$7F:F000` shifted by 4 slots, puts the word's four 16x16 sprites in slots 0-3 (highest priority, so the per-scanline sprite limit drops game sprites, not the word), and points the OAM DMA at the buffer for that frame. The game's own buffer is untouched. First frame copies all slots; later frames only the first 64 (the game uses at most ~42) to keep the cost low (measured ~2% slower game in a busy boss scene while a pop-up shows). `$7F:F000-F21F` was never written by the game (power-on `$55`).
- **Top-right copy** (BG3 row 2, columns 22-31) was removed in v29 at the author's request (the sprite pop-up is the only event text). The code is still in tools/ninja.asm but never runs: ShowMsg no longer sets `$077F`.
- RAM: `$077E/$077F` top-right type/timer; `$0780` pop-up type, `$0781` timer, `$0782` x, `$0783` start y, `$0784` upload pending, `$0787` HUD "!" glyph shown; scratch `$0774-$0775`.
- ShowMsg reads the object's screen position from `[$00]+4` (x) / `[$00]+6` (y) via the object pointer in direct page `$00` at the event. asar gotcha found here: with M=16 an immediate like `#!PopRise+24` is assembled as 8-bit unless written `.w`, which desyncs the code; use explicit `.w`/`.b`.
- HUD BG3 facts: tilemap word `$5C00`, 2bpp font at char base 0 (digits `$A1-$AA`, `A`-`Z` `$B2-$CB`, no "!" so the top-right text writes one into blank tile `$E1`). Bosses show BG3 dialogue boxes and an "ENEMY" bar, so BG3 pop-ups (v20/v21) were dropped.
- (Fixed in v31: an evade no longer plays any hit reaction; see the Ninja section.)

## Bugs fixed in v19
- **Freeze when recruiting Big Man (v17/v18):** the Ninja dodge code added to `AbsorbHook` in bank 81 pushed hack.asm's code past `$81:FF80`, where recruit.asm then wrote `RecruitBonus`, overwriting `BigInit`/`BigPend`. Fixed by moving the dodge to `$82:FA00` (`NinjaDodge`). `scripts/ovl.py`-style check: assemble each patch separately and make sure no two patches write the same byte (apart from the header checksum).
- **Big Man joined 2 levels too high when you had no ally (since the first Big Man build):** `BigInit` overwrote A before calling `$01:8432`, which compares A (new ally id) with `$0424`; with no ally both were 0, so it took the "same ally again: +2 levels" path. Fixed by preserving A. Verified on the floor-0 door: original L2, v18/older L3, v19 L2; with the Amazon as ally the Recruiter bonus applies.
- Free space map now: bank 81 `$81:FE00-FE9A` Priest, `$81:FF00-FF7F` hack (AbsorbHook/BigInit/BigPend), `$81:FF80` Recruit, `$81:FFC0` Big Man regen; bank 82 `$82:EE00` hints, `$82:F200` Wizard, `$82:F400` Crit, `$82:F480` NinjaInv, `$82:F600-F8xx` messages, `$89:B000` pop-up graphics, `$82:FA00` NinjaDodge; bank 08 `$08:F600` music fix (PlayCmd); bank 89 `$89:A929` Second Wind.

## Attract-loop ally descriptions (v24)
- Text is plain ASCII in ROM (`$00` = new line, `$FF` = end); the originals are at `$07:B1A0-B49B`. Each screen has a long pointer pair (name sprite list, text) in the table at `$00:D813` (8 entries x 6 bytes, order: Big Man, Knight, Ninja, Cleric, Amazon, Wizard, Lizardman, Thief), so new text can live anywhere.
- v24/v25 (tools/text.asm) put new text for Big Man, Amazon, Cleric, Wizard, Ninja and Thief at `$89:C000` and repoints their text pointers; Knight and Lizardman keep the original text. Format: weapon first, then the perk in plain words, no numbers. Limits: lines of up to ~25-26 characters, 6 lines fit above the sprite (the original Lizardman uses 6). The font has capitals, digits, period, comma and apostrophe.
- Verified by capturing every description screen from the demo loop (all 8 render fully, nothing overlaps the sprite or LEVEL).

## THIEF VISION option (v27; one build replaces the Flicker/Translucent pair)
- OPTION MODE gets a new item "THIEF VISION" with values FLICKER / DITHER, between SOUND TEST and EXIT. Stored in `$04A4` (0 = FLICKER, 1 = DITHER; never touched by the original game on any screen or floor checked), set to DITHER (v28; FLICKER in v27) by the power-on option defaults (`$00:DFD4` hook), kept across games like the button setup.
- Menu internals: items are 0 game level, 1-3 attack/jump/special, 4 music test, 5 sound test, 6 (new) THIEF VISION, 7 EXIT (was 6). Cursor `$13A1`, previous item `$13A2` (setting it to EXIT forces a redraw), highlight flag `$13A9`, option values `$13A3+item` (the generic left/right code; item 6 is handled separately because `$13A9` sits there). Per-item draw routines via a jump table (was `$00:E4ED`, now `OptTable` at `$80:F76C`); each sets `$13AB` to a draw list in bank 07: `[length word][VRAM word address, tile word]...`, tile = ASCII + `$71`, attr `$30` red / `$20` green (highlighted). Static labels: list at `$07:CD05` (`$94` bytes); value lists via the table at `$00:E652`.
- Layout: SNES text sits on an 8-pixel grid, so "1.5 spacing" is not possible; instead every label now sits directly above its value with a blank row between groups (rows: GAME LEVEL 6/7, CONTROL PAD 9, ATTACK/JUMP/SPECIAL 10-12, MUSIC TEST 14/15, SOUND TEST 17/18, THIEF VISION 20/21, EXIT 23). Horizontally the original menu centres every label and value on column 15.5, so v28 puts THIEF VISION at columns 10-21, FLICKER at 12-18 and DITHER at 13-18 (v27 had them left-aligned at column 11). All 151 VRAM addresses in the option lists plus the music/sound digit positions were remapped by script (tools/options.asm).
- Hint code (tools/hack_opt.asm, based on the dither version): FLICKER mode skips the draw on off-phase frames and uses a full-graphics table (`HintTableF`, ids `$7E20+`, graphics at `$16:B900`); DITHER mode is unchanged. Verified both modes on the floor-7 trap door, and that the option survives starting a game.

## Save system (v30)
**Decisions (agreed Nov 8):** 3 slots in battery SRAM; slot screen after title START; empty slot -> normal new game, used slot -> resume (Y yes / B no), then overwrite prompt; autosave at the start of every floor; on game over the save stays at the last floor reached; life is saved as is (not refilled); options (buttons, THIEF VISION) to be saved globally; no copy/erase for now; a name (up to 6 letters, RPG style) per slot shown at the top of its panel.
**Future idea (author):** a high-score table ("HALL OF VALIENTS" in the arcade) with the slot names; a saved game on the last level may enter a high score only once.

**Findings:**
- The whole run state lives in WRAM `$0403-$0491` and carries over between floors (the scene-load RAM clear at `$80:8C21` starts at `$0500`): floor `$0403` (+ section `$0404/$0405`), ally `$0424` / level `$0412` / per-type levels `$0413-$041A`, keys `$040F-$0411`, lives `$0406`, life `$0431` / max `$042B`, score `$0441-$0448`, passive-item slot `$0427` (type) / `$0428` (floors left; the floor start moves it to `$040E`), King's staff MAGIC countdown `$043D-$0440`, etc. New-game init is `$80:CD85-CDE5` + `$80:CBF6` (life 160/160). Options live in `$0495-$0498` (buttons), `$04A4` (THIEF VISION); `$049B` = highest floor reached.
- Floor-start routine: `$80:82EF` (runs once at the start of every floor, after the floor's data loads; not on a death respawn). Start-point menu object: cursor `$045F`, floor table `$80:BFA1` (0,5,9,13,22,26,30,33), confirm at `$80:BF8D`.
- The game never writes to SRAM (checked: 40,000 frames of title/demo and 3,000 frames on floors 4, 18, 30, 37, 45 with SRAM enabled: 0 bytes changed).

**Core (tools/save.asm):** header = ROM+RAM+battery, 2 KB. Slot n at `$70:0n00`: +0 marker `$A5`, +1..+6 name, +`$0E` checksum (16-bit sum), +`$10..$9E` = `$0403-$0491`. `!Slot` = `$04A5` (active slot), `!Resume` = `$04A6`. Hook `$80:82EF`: resume -> copy the slot back to WRAM (skipping the frame counter `$0460`), else autosave the block. Hook `$80:BF7C`: while resuming, the start-point menu takes floor/section from the slot and confirms at once (the level data loads before `$82EF`, so the floor must be set here). Verified: autosave at the floor 7->8 start, then a cold boot resume lands on floor 8 with the Thief L2, life 88, keys and score intact.

**Slot screen and options (tools/slot.asm, v30):**
- Title START sets `$04A3 = $C3` (magic value; power-on RAM is random) and continues to scene 4; the scene dispatcher hook (`$80:8198` -> `Dispatch` at `$80:F8A4`) sees the flag and runs `SlotScene` (`$89:C40E`) instead. Dispatch also clears `!Slot/!Resume` whenever the boot/title/attract scenes (0-2) start, so the attract demo never autosaves.
- The screen reuses the OPTION MODE set-up routines (tower background `$C4E5/$C515/$C649`, font `$E3CB`, palette `$E637`, fade in `$81C6/$8211`, fade out `$8257/$826F`, pad read `$89D5`) through a trampoline `CallLow` (`$80:F8A0`: `JSR ($04A7,X)` / `RTL`). Text is drawn into a BG3 shadow tilemap at `$7F:E000` that is DMA'd to VRAM `$5C00` every frame; UI state at `$7F:E700+`.
- Panels (stacked, full width): slot number + name + FLOOR nn (`$0404`); game level + ally name/level + CREDITS (`$042D`); SCORE (7 digits). The HUD's left number is not lives: it is the count of full 80-point life bars (`$0406`), the rest in `$0407` (`$00:B067`). Ally id/level and credits are captured after the floor set-up has chosen the ally (hook `$80:83D0` -> slot +`$A0..$A2`, display only), because the save itself happens before that.
- Buttons follow OPTION MODE: confirm = the configured attack button (or START), back/no = the configured jump button; the footer shows the letters (masks from the game's table `$80:E154`). Used slot: CONTINUE THIS ADVENTURE? yes = resume, no = START A NEW GAME HERE? yes = name entry (prefilled with the old name), no = back. Empty slot: name entry (default HERO; up/down letter, left/right or attack/jump move, START done, jump on the first letter cancels). Starting a new game clears the slot's valid marker until its first autosave. Jump on the list returns to the title.
- Saved options at SRAM `$70:0010` (+0 `$5A`, +1 game level `$0409`, +2..+5 buttons `$0495-$0498`, +6 THIEF VISION `$04A4`, +7 checksum): written when leaving OPTION MODE (hook `$80:E122`), loaded at power-on after the defaults (PowerOnHook -> `LoadOptions` `$89:F000`), which also rebuilds the button masks `$0464/$0466/$0468` the way the game does (the unassigned face button also attacks).
- Tests: new game with a name -> autosave -> power cycle -> slot shows the run -> resume; resume of a floor-8 save made in real play (Thief L2, life, score restored); game over plus 9,000 frames of title/attract changed 0 SRAM bytes; a corrupted slot shows EMPTY; with attack = X and jump = A in the options the screen uses X/A; options survive a power cycle (HARD, X/B/A, FLICKER); regressions (Big Man recruit, music, pop-ups, THIEF VISION, option menu) pass.
- Not done / ideas: ally head icons on the slot screen (text names for now), copy/erase, high-score table.

## Save screen and high scores (v31-v49; tools/slot.asm, hiscore.asm, starbg.inc)
**Slot screen (agreed Nov 8-9):** three stacked boxes on the floor-select tower background, three lines each:
`NAME  FLOOR 08  NORMAL` / `L2 LIZARDMAN  CREDITS 1` / `SCORE 0151640  LIVES 2.0`. FLOOR is centred, difficulty/credits/lives right-justified, lives always decimal (and "10" at the 800 cap, not 10.0), HP rejected (does not match the HUD), credits kept (continues are limited to 3). Ally portrait in each box (HUD portraits at `$9F:C000 + 128*index`). Cursor sound on slot up/down only (SndTick drains the sound queue because no objects run); no confirm sound; letter changes silent. Bright white means selected or needs attention (EMPTY is white only for the selected slot). Blank name -> HERO; starting over in a used slot prefills the old name; SPECIAL deletes a save (with a confirm); resume skips the floor select. ## Switching-loop brainstorm (Nov 12) - decisions so far
- **Data behind it** (allies_per_floor.csv, ally_level_table.csv, ally_regen.csv, ally_shot_damage.csv): join level comes from `$07:F1B0[floor*8+type]`. Floors 0-7 join at L1-L2, 15-20 at L3-L5, 23+ at L5-L8, 33+ at L8 for almost everyone. Freeing your current type again gives +2 (cap L8), so common allies (Ninja 11 doors, Wizard 10) snowball to L8 by floor 15-20. Switching only really costs something in the mid game (floors ~8-28). Amazon doors: 0, 2, 3, 17, 33, 55; Thief doors: 2, 7, 20, 39, 42. Never more than one of a type per floor.
- **Rejected:** flat or partial level carry-over and catch-up leveling (make levels meaningless); "allies remember you" (a nerf to levels, not a player choice); ally fatigue.
- **Amazon carrier (planned).** Author's line to remember: *"Early on, the bonus is her reason to use her, and later the carry is."* Simplified rule (replaces the +1/+2/+3 Recruiter scaling):
  - When she joins: if your previous ally's level is higher than her join level, she takes that level.
  - When you swap her for a new ally: if the new ally's join level is lower than hers, it takes her level; if it is the same or higher, it gets +1 (cap L8).
  - Same as: new ally level = max(Amazon level, table level + 1), capped at L8. Freeing an Amazon while you have one is still the normal +2.
- **Door swaps (planned):** one more mid-game Amazon and Thief (late teens / 20s). Candidates: Amazon on 21 or 27 (replacing a weak Knight), Thief on 18 (traps + fairy) or 23.
- **Big Man (v59):** regen curve made steep instead of flat: 13.3 s per block at L1 down to 4.0 s at L8 (row `$01:8AE6` = 800..240 frames), and the 1/4-speed tick hook removed. The revive-once idea is held in reserve. README/description screen to say he heals faster as he levels.

No ally fires unless you attack. Big Man, Knight, Lizardman not measured (no usable test state; the bm0/bm1/coin states stay frozen).
