# Between-level messages by floor

Shown on the NEXT FLOOR screen as you arrive on that floor (floor 17 = the screen before you enter floor 17). Msg # is the number in `between_level_messages.md`. Text is what v64 shows now. `/` = line break, `␣` = a blank line. The dialog fits **12 characters per line and 7 lines**.

In v65 the **BOSS AFTER THIS FLOOR!** messages on floors 3, 11, 23, 35 and 48 are built, and in v106 floor 28 (before the Emerald Dragon) (new text is stored in free bank $86). The boss floors' own screens keep their original ominous text, since a warning there comes too late to act on. Floors 0, 1, 2 and 17 share one message, and floors 16 and 30 share another. Floor 16 keeps its original text because the SNES version has no boss on floor 17.
