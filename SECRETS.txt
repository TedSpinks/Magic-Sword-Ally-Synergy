Magic Sword: Ally Synergy - secrets
===================================

SPOILER WARNING: this file spoils a hidden feature. You don't need it to
enjoy the patch. Stop reading here if you'd rather find it on your own.


Start with an ally
------------------
When you start a new game, enter an ally's name as your name and you'll
start the run with that ally:

   AMAZON   BIGMAN   WIZARD   KNIGHT
   LIZARD   NINJA    THIEF    CLERIC

- The ally joins at their normal level for the floor you start on.
- It works from the BEGINNING and from any other starting floor.
- Your name stays as you typed it on the save screen, but you can change
  it for the score table.
